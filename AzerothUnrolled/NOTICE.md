What the add-on contains or uses from the game:

- **Blizzard art is drawn from your game client** - map art, minimap art and UI icons, by file ID or atlas name, at
  run time - with one exception (1.3.0, the minimap imagery): the 153 pictures in `Media\Minimap\` are scaled-down
  composites of the client's own continent minimap tiles (16, 32 and 64 px per tile, joined into 256 px pages, the
  open sea recoloured), generated by the author's tools. They are Blizzard's art, reduced; the GPL above does not
  cover them. And (1.3.1) the 18 pictures in `Media\Gilneas\`: a map page of Gilneas, which the client has as terrain
  but draws no map of, in two looks - explored and unexplored -, drawn by the author's tools from the client's terrain
  (its heights, water, buildings, road texture and minimap colours) with its mountain ranges pieced together from the
  drawn ranges of Blizzard's Silverpine Forest map (its explored and its unexplored drawing) and in its colours. They
  contain Blizzard's art, rearranged; the GPL above does not cover them.
- **IDs, names and numbers read from the client's data tables** (map, uiMap, area, encounter and file IDs;
  instance, floor, room and encounter names in English; map rectangles and world positions of doors and
  portals; explored-area overlay rectangles). These are in the `.lua` data files.
- **Shapes derived from the client's art and world geometry**: the 783 alpha masks in `Masks\` (49 zone and city
  outlines computed from the client's zone highlight and map tiles, and 4 patch masks of Orgrimmar and Stormwind; two
  Blackrock Mountain outlines cut from the client's cave map art; the Dalaran barrier ring computed from minimap
  tiles; 32 cave outlines cut from the client's cave maps and 260 drawn from its minimap of the caves; 428 masks of the
  open sea on the coastal minimap tiles; Gilneas's outline and the 6 outlines of its areas computed from the client's
  terrain and its area IDs) and the room outlines in
  `SubZoneRegions.lua` (computed from the client's building geometry). They are outlines only, generated by
  the author's tools; they remain derived from Blizzard's material.
- **Instances that are not released yet** appear as grey placeholders. Their names, level ranges and
  approximate spots come from the beta client's data and public guides; they may be wrong and may change.

## 3. Data researched from third parties

Only derived values (a position on a map page, a level range) are stored in the add-on; no third-party
database extract, guide text or image is included.
