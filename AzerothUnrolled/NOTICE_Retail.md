What the add-on contains or uses from the game:

- **Blizzard art is drawn from your game client** - the world, continent and zone maps and UI icons, by file ID or
  atlas name, at run time - with these exceptions, all generated by the author's tools from the client's own art. They
  are Blizzard's art, reduced or reworked; the GPL above does not cover them:
  - the minimap imagery: the 1,107 pictures in `Media\Retail\Minimap\` are scaled-down composites of the client's
    minimap tiles (joined into 256 px pages, the open sea recoloured; for Vashj'ir the client's sea-floor tiles);
  - the zones' small pictures: the 488 pictures in `Masks\Retail\Lod\` are each a zone's map art with its explored
    pieces, reduced to 512 px;
  - the covers of printed names: the 107 pictures in `Masks\Retail\Covers\` paint a name out of a map from the art
    round it.
- **IDs, names and numbers read from the client's data tables** (map, uiMap, area, journal instance and file IDs;
  map rectangles, the maps' links and groups, door positions, flight-master maps, the phases' maps, the travel graph's
  places and names, the explored tiles with nothing in them). These are in the `.lua` data files (`RetailTree.lua`,
  `RetailCellData.lua`, `RetailPlaces.lua`, `RetailCaveData.lua`, `RetailEmptyTiles.lua`, `RetailLodData.lua`,
  `RetailMinimap*.lua`).
- **Shapes derived from the client's art**: the 1,341 alpha masks in `Masks\Retail\` (each continent's land, each
  zone's cut-out and the city patches, computed from the client's map art and zone highlights; the caves' and
  interiors' outlines from their micro maps and indoor minimaps; the open sea on the coastal minimap tiles; the shapes
  of the printed names; a few round and soft-edged shapes). They are outlines only, generated by the author's tools;
  they remain derived from Blizzard's material.
- **Icons of the add-on's own**: `Masks\Retail\portal.tga`, `boat.tga`, `zeppelin.tga`, `tram.tga` and `spark.tga`, drawn by
  the author's tools, are under the licence above.
